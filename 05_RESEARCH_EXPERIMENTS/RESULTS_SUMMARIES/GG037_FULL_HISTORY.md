### GG-037 — Calibration Collapse + Inverted Edge Realization: the Model's Edge Signal Is Anti-Predictive

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Gap ID | GG-037 |
| Discovered | 2026-06-16 SESSION 64 — full-database assessment of the re-frozen 158-bet NBA Finals dataset (`data/nba_finals_clean_dataset.csv`), run after the GG-036/GG-035 sprint. Directly confirms the audit-directive prime suspect "calibration collapse." |
| Category | Model-validity / forecast-integrity gap — the C3 forecast is severely overconfident, and the derived `fee_net_edge` gate (the core EV decision rule) is **negatively correlated** with realized outcomes on the only resolved data we have. Distinct from [[GG-004]] (which is about *sample adequacy* for the commercial claim); this is about the *direction and calibration* of the signal on the sample we already hold. |
| What was missed | No governance artifact asked whether the model's probabilities are **calibrated** or whether **higher claimed edge actually produces better outcomes.** The full database says no on both counts: (1) **Calibration collapse** — directional Brier 0.2308 (barely beats 0.25 coinflip); mean stated confidence 0.799 vs actual win rate 0.601 (**≈20-point overconfidence**); every confidence bucket ≥0.7 is materially overconfident; **72 of 158 bets (46%) sit in the [0.90,1.00) bucket predicting 0.970 but realizing 0.792 (−$108.19).** (2) **Inverted edge realization** — within the YES-frame positive-edge bands, `fee_net_edge`[0.00,0.05) → wr 0.714 / **+$15.46**; [0.05,0.10) → wr 0.529 / **−$78.08**; [0.10,0.20) → wr 0.368 / **−$119.93**; [0.20,1.0) → wr 0.500 / −$10.74. **The bets where the model claims the largest mispricing lose the most.** High claimed edge clusters at mean `model_p`≈0.74–0.76 — i.e. apparent "edge" is the same overconfidence, not real alpha; the market is closer to truth precisely where the model cries mispricing. |
| Mechanism (fully traced) | Overconfident `model_p` → inflated `gross/fee_net_edge` → larger Kelly stake → systematic loss. This also explains the headline paradox: **60.1% win rate yet −$248.90 net** (−7.28% ROI on $3,420 turnover, profit factor 0.742). Payoff is asymmetric — avg win $7.53 vs avg loss −$15.31 (payoff ratio 0.492) → **breakeven win rate needed = 0.670, actual only 0.601.** The bot wins often but small and loses big (fading at short prices), and the Kelly stake is largest exactly on the worst-calibrated high-confidence bets. |
| When it should have been caught | At any point a reliability diagram / edge-decile P&L curve was produced. None was — all prior EV work measured against `mip=0.50` ([[GG-004]]/KI-017) or reported aggregate win rate, neither of which exposes calibration or edge monotonicity. |
| Phases it survived | G2–G5 paper trading. The 60% headline win rate read as "healthy" in self-reported summaries — the exact audit-directive thesis (self-reported health > reality). |
| Why it survived | No monitor for calibration (predicted vs realized by bucket) and no monitor for edge-realization monotonicity. C7 watches drawdown/drift, not whether the probability estimates mean what they claim. |
| Impact | **HIGH (commercial-thesis-level).** This is direct evidence that, on the only resolved data in existence, the model does **not** beat the market and its edge signal is **anti-predictive** — the core EV claim (`model_p > mip + vig`) is not just unproven ([[GG-004]]) but pointing the wrong way where the stake is largest. It does not by itself prove the strategy fails (see caveat), but it places a heavy, specific burden on EXP-017 at scale and is a G6 blocker alongside GG-004. |
| Honesty caveat (ADR-0024) | **n=158, single series (NBA Finals NYK), T1-only, ~20–30 players.** Per ADR-0024 this is *structurally insufficient* for a commercial verdict (floor: n≥500 unique markets, ≥15 opponents, or ≥2 sports each ≥150). These are **strong directional RED FLAGS, not statistical proof.** A Finals series is a high-variance, low-sample, correlated regime. The finding is "the available evidence is damning and the signal looks inverted," not "the strategy is proven to lose." |
| Discovery | SESSION 64 — post-sprint full-database assessment (this session). |
