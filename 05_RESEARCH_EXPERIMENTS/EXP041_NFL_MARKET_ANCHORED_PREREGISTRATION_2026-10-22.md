## 3. The gate (as accepted; ADR-0072 amendment text is Block 85.2's)
1. **One position per market per band.** 2. **The model is pulled toward the market by its measured skill:** w_family above (held-out Brier improvement over the anchor; zero means "price = market"). 3. **An order goes in only when what is left clears fee + the model's measured error:** `|w × (adjusted − anchor)| × max(λ̂ − SE(λ̂), 0) > ceil(0.07 × p × (1 − p) × 100)/100`, with λ̂ the held-out slope of (outcome − anchor) on (adjusted − anchor) and its bootstrap SE, from the frozen params file (ML λ̂ 1.24, SE 2.10; SPREAD 0.41, 0.58; TOTAL −0.42, 0.37; TEAMTOTAL 0.21, 0.38). 4. **A game's ladder counts as one exposure:** the strike with the largest net edge only. Sources: all gate parameters are measured on 2020-2025 out-of-sample predictions; none is chosen from outcomes of the judged period. Part A: **0 of 18,479 historical strikes cleared this gate**, so the registered expectation is zero or near-zero orders; the cell is judged on logged forecasts (shadow), not fills.

## 4. THE POOLED TEST
- **One NFL test, all 12 cells, clustered by game.** Unit = game. Per game, per family, mean over its strikes of Brier(price) − Brier(anchor); per-game pooled value = mean of the four family means (equal family weights); playoffs excluded.
- **Primary metric:** pooled paired ΔBrier (price − anchor), game-clustered bootstrap B=2000, seed 20261022. BEATS if the one-sided 95% upper bound < 0; HARMS if the lower bound > 0 (two-sided 95% for HARMS); else NOT DISTINGUISHABLE.
- **Many-cells correction:** the pooled test is the single primary (α = 0.05, one-sided, no correction needed for one test). The 12 cells and 4 families are secondary: Holm across 12 at family-wise 0.05, reported only; a cell may not be promoted on its own.
- **Judged n (power calc on Part A's measured effect):** measured pooled effect +0.00002 [−0.00014, +0.00017] (held-out, n=1,087 games): zero or wrong sign, so the n needed to detect the measured effect is unbounded. Planning n uses the largest effect ever observed for this factor class, F8 closing-line ΔBrier −0.0007 (W77.2), with the pooled per-game SD at w=1 of 0.0063: n = (1.645+0.842)² × (0.0063/0.0007)² ≈ **500 games**. At 272 regular-season games per year, and a start only after wiring, that is **two seasons: not before the end of the 2027 season (~Jan 2028)**. **No scored look below n = 500 games**; the 2026 season alone cannot reach it.
- **Looks:** (a) **Descriptive count only, 2027-01-10** (end of 2026 regular season): rows logged, anchor-vs-close gap by horizon, no score. (b) **Scored look at 500 games with logged price**, expected after Week 18 of the 2027 season (~2028-01-09); if wiring slips, the look date slips and n does not change.
