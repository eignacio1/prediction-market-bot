## What this project is

**A calibration engine for probabilistic reasoning, structured as a trading bot.** Prediction markets are the laboratory: binary
outcomes, short feedback loops, and the market's own probability as an explicit benchmark. **P&L is the score; the methodology is the
asset.** Kalshi is the venue; MLB, NFL, NBA and NHL are the sports. Operator: **Earl Ignacio**.

### The architecture — every route optimized by layered multi-arm trees (ADR-0077, DOCTRINE §1)

> "my vision from DAY 1 was to build out the decision trees so we optimize every single route while using tried and true math scaffolding
> and betting architecture. Does a quant - that finds through rigorous testing and scaffolding that a certain betting pattern doesn't beat
> the "market" keep going? no he fucking routes it to the correct path so that loss still WINS." — operator, 2026-11-10 21:41 PT

- For every market a tree picks the **arm: ride the market, fade it, momentum, pot of gold, or no bet.** Layer 1 market state (price,
  move, ladder gap, book, time to start) · layer 2 filters from the depth factors (matchup, lineup / usage, rest / travel, refs, weather,
  PAM, qualitative) · layer 3 a tree per cell choosing the arm, depth set by horizon · then risk / sizing and execution.
- The engine's simulated probability is **one input** to the tree, never the bet. Depth is "meant to ENHANCE OUR DECISION TREES WHICH COULD
  VERY WELL MEAN RIDE THE WAVE" (21:35 PT).
- **"Does not beat the market" is a routing result** — it tells the tree which arm that leaf gets — never a stop. Frozen tests judge cells
  and leaves; they never gate building.
- **A factor is applied when it is a tested split in the tree**, scored per leaf: net after costs, held out, per arm. Model-vs-market Brier
  may be reported last; it is never the verdict.
- `model_p > market + vig` is the **fade arm's entry rule**, nothing more.
- **Words:** never "no edge" (operator 21:56 PT: "WE ARE SO FAR AWAY"). A losing pattern is "routed to ride / no bet in these leaves".
  **A losing look closes only that test** — "THAT SPECIFIC HYPOTHESIS, for future research and development that we'll circle back. ... a
  cell closing shouldn't mean anything is being closed but a test" (11-11 16:51 PT). No cell, market or arm closes; text says
  "hypothesis closed (EXP-id, parked for R&D)".

### What winning looks like — the operator's north star (four pillars)

*The single canonical statement; other files cite this heading. Operator 2026-10-08: "We're trying to fucking win."*

**The bot running at optimum:** every live sport covering its whole open board — spreads / run lines, totals and team totals, moneylines,
props and parlays — across all five bands (T4 with its sub-bands), each cell routed to its best arm per leaf by its own pre-registered
measurement.

**Market classes (DOCTRINE §6, operator 09-25, 10-16, 11-05).** The pot of gold — big hitters, longshots, parlays — **is an arm of the same
tree now, not a later phase**: "we should have the pot of gold decision trees routed in too" (11-05). It is built alongside the standard
board (Wave 110 built the same-game correlation it needs). The hunt starts in retail-priced flow before institutionally priced headline
lines: "beating buddy off his phone placing parlays, that's the first step" (10-16). The opportunity is accumulation across every arm —
"picking up every damn cent that falls along the way" — and the pot of gold is one of the arms that picks it up.

| Pillar | What it means | Measures (values only in the RUNNING SITREP) |
|---|---|---|
| **BREADTH** | The whole board has an arm logging. | **B1** registered cells logging (ACTIVE + SHADOW) / registered · **B2** families with a logging cell · **B3** bands with a logging cell (T4 sub-bands named) · **B4** props and parlay families logging |

feeds, governance, dashboards) is necessary and is not a pillar; two consecutive waves that move no pillar is drift, flagged unprompted.

### The scale and what edge means (ADR-0073, ADR-0075, GRANULARITY_INDEX.md)

- **One deep engine per sport** at Syndicate32-level granularity or deeper (a bar, not an endorsement — "there's a clear bar that we
  aren't even remotely close to getting to"): finest event-level data → every factor class built once, point-in-time → one game
  simulation pricing the whole board, parlay correlations included. Free data first ($0); a paid-only factor is a registered gap. NFL is
  the template, NBA the speed lane, then NHL, MLB. PAM (Player Attribute Matrix) is piped into every engine.
- **Edge = the betting architecture, not a slogan** (operator 10-14: "I don't just want to dilute it to something as simple as 'no more
  generality go edge' - it's not that simple and should never be treated as such"). Data aggregation > interpretation > **application**
  ("cool info, so what?"), at the granularity of "what teams run the most cover 6 against 3 wide out sets that's motioned to the
  boundary, matched with volume and success rate". Eight components per sport: decision trees, filtering mechanisms, cluster designs,
  cross-reference compatibility, momentum looping, temporal evolution, structural tensions ("where the architecture strains"), seat
  interface ("deep mapping of sports-eyes to cells").
- **Horizon (ADR-0068):** tree depth is governed by decision horizon — the longer the horizon, the deeper the tree; a shallow tree must be
  paid for in measured speed. Pre-game first, then carry the granularity inward.
- **Throughput:** design for hundreds to ~1,000 evaluations per game, routed per bet, capital-ceiling agnostic; kill switches per band and
  system-wide, independent. Anything that only works for a handful of bets per night is architecturally wrong.

When the operator asks for the vision, the scale or the bar, read it back in his words (`/vision`). Never compress his words into a slogan.
